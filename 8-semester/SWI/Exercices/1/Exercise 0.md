**Exercise 0** is all about innovation opportunities for you this semester.

Look at the semester project description in the study regulations for your education. See if your topic is related to one or more of the following trends and forces:

- Do you see trends in _technology_ that might be related to your topic?
- Do you see trends in _legislation_, _society_, _culture_, and _economy_ that might be linked to your topic?
- Do you see new _forces_ in industry and markets, new _needs_ and _demands_, new _products_, and new _conditions_ relevant to your topic?
- How can such trends and forces create _innovation opportunities_ in your project?
