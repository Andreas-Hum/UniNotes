**Exercise 1** is a follow-up to **Exercise 0**. In this exercise, we will consider your semester project:

- What _problem_ are you working on?
- Explain what _type_ of innovation you aim for (Table 1.1 in Essence).
- What will be the _keystones_ in your design? What will make your design stand out?
- Who will _benefit_ from it? What value will you create?
- Where will it be _used_? How will it create _change_?
- Characterize your problem or solution challenges in terms of _volatility_, _uncertainty_, _complexity_, and _ambiguity_ (Section 1.2 in Essence).
- Do you consider your project to be mainly _demand-pull_ or _technology-push_? (Section 1.2 in Essence).
- Will _roles_ be relevant? How? When? What are the pros and cons of roles?
