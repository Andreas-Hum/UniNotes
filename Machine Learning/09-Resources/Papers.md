# Landmark Papers

Search arXiv or Semantic Scholar by title.

## Classical
- Rosenblatt (1958) *The Perceptron*
- Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams (1986) *Learning representations by back-propagating errors*
- Cortes & Vapnik (1995) *Support-Vector Networks*
- Breiman (2001) *Random Forests*; Friedman (2001) *Greedy Function Approximation: Gradient Boosting*
- Dempster, Laird, Rubin (1977) *Maximum Likelihood from Incomplete Data via the EM Algorithm*
- Hochreiter & Schmidhuber (1997) *Long Short-Term Memory*
- Tipping & Bishop (1999) *Probabilistic PCA*

## Deep learning
- Krizhevsky et al. (2012) *AlexNet*
- He et al. (2015) *Deep Residual Learning (ResNet)*
- Ioffe & Szegedy (2015) *Batch Normalization*
- Srivastava et al. (2014) *Dropout*
- Kingma & Ba (2014) *Adam*
- Vaswani et al. (2017) *Attention Is All You Need*
- Devlin et al. (2018) *BERT*; Brown et al. (2020) *GPT-3*
- Dosovitskiy et al. (2020) *ViT*
- Kingma & Welling (2013) *Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes*
- Goodfellow et al. (2014) *Generative Adversarial Nets*
- Ho, Jain, Abbeel (2020) *Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models*
- Hu et al. (2021) *LoRA*

## RL
- Watkins & Dayan (1992) *Q-learning*
- Mnih et al. (2015) *Human-level control through deep RL (DQN)*
- Schulman et al. (2017) *Proximal Policy Optimization*
- Silver et al. (2016) *Mastering the game of Go*

## Graphs
- Perozzi et al. (2014) *DeepWalk*; Grover & Leskovec (2016) *node2vec*
- Kipf & Welling (2016) *Semi-Supervised Classification with GCNs*
- Hamilton et al. (2017) *GraphSAGE*; Veličković et al. (2018) *GAT*
- Xu et al. (2019) *How Powerful are GNNs? (GIN)*
- Zhang & Chen (2018) *Link Prediction Based on GNNs (SEAL)*

## Reading tips
Read abstract → figures → conclusions → method. Use Papers With Code, Connected Papers, and Distill.
