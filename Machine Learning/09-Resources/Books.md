# Books

★ = free online. Ordered roughly from beginner to advanced within each section.

## Core ML
- ★ [**An Introduction to Statistical Learning** (ISLR / ISLP, Python edition)](https://www.statlearning.com/) — James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani. *Best first book.*
- ★ [**The Elements of Statistical Learning**](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/) — Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman. The rigorous big brother of ISL.
- ★ [**Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning**](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/pattern-recognition-machine-learning/) — C. Bishop (2006). Probabilistic view; your course book (copy in `8-semester/ML/Books`).
- ★ [**Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction / Advanced Topics**](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/) — K. Murphy (2022/2023).
- *Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms* — Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David (theory, PAC learning).
- *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow* — A. Géron (practical).
- *Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn* — S. Raschka.
- *The Hundred-Page Machine Learning Book* — A. Burkov.

## Math for ML
- ★ [**Mathematics for Machine Learning**](https://mml-book.github.io/) — Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong.
- *Linear Algebra and Learning from Data* — G. Strang.
- *Convex Optimization* — Boyd & Vandenberghe.
- *All of Statistics* — L. Wasserman.

## Deep learning
- ★ [**Neural Networks and Deep Learning**](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/) — M. Nielsen. *Gentlest intro.*
- ★ [**Dive into Deep Learning (d2l.ai)**](https://d2l.ai/) — Zhang, Lipton, Li, Smola. Interactive with PyTorch code.
- ★ [**Understanding Deep Learning**](https://udlbook.github.io/udlbook/) — S. Prince (2023). Modern, beautifully illustrated.
- ★ [**Deep Learning**](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville (2016). Classic theory reference.
- *Deep Learning with Python* — F. Chollet.

## Specialised
- ★ [**Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction**](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) — Sutton & Barto.
- ★ [**Graph Representation Learning**](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~wlh/grl_book/) — W. Hamilton.
- ★ [**Speech and Language Processing**](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/) — Jurafsky & Martin (NLP + LLMs).
- ★ [**Mining of Massive Datasets**](http://www.mmds.org/) — Leskovec, Rajaraman, Ullman (recommenders, LSH, graphs).
- [**Recommender Systems Handbook**](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-0716-2197-4) — Ricci et al.; *Recommender Systems: The Textbook* — Aggarwal.
- ★ [**Forecasting: Principles and Practice**](https://otexts.com/fpp3/) — Hyndman & Athanasopoulos.
- ★ [**Interpretable Machine Learning**](https://christophm.github.io/interpretable-ml-book/) — C. Molnar.
- ★ [**Fairness and Machine Learning**](https://fairmlbook.org/) — Barocas, Hardt, Narayanan.
- *Designing Machine Learning Systems* — C. Huyen (MLOps).
- *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms* — D. MacKay (free from the author).
- *Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning* — Rasmussen & Williams (free from the authors).
