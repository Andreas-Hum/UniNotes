# Books

## Core textbooks
- **Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning** — C. Bishop (2006). Probabilistic view; a PDF is already in `8-semester/ML/Books`.
- **Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective** — K. Murphy (2012); successor **Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction / Advanced Topics** (2022/2023, free online).
- **The Elements of Statistical Learning** — Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman (free PDF).
- **An Introduction to Statistical Learning (ISLR / ISLP)** — gentle, with R/Python labs (free).
- **Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms** — Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David (theory, PAC; free).
- **Foundations of Machine Learning** — Mohri et al.
- **Pattern Classification** — Duda, Hart, Stork.
- **Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms** — D. MacKay (free).
- **Bayesian Reasoning and Machine Learning** — D. Barber (free).
- **Gaussian Processes for Machine Learning** — Rasmussen & Williams (free).

## Deep learning
- **Deep Learning** — Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville (free at deeplearningbook.org).
- **Dive into Deep Learning (d2l.ai)** — interactive, code in PyTorch/JAX (free).
- **Understanding Deep Learning** — S. Prince (2023, free).
- **Neural Networks and Deep Learning** — M. Nielsen (free online, great intro).
- **Deep Learning with Python** — F. Chollet.

## Reinforcement learning
- **Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction** — Sutton & Barto (free PDF).
- **Algorithms for Reinforcement Learning** — Szepesvári (free).

## Graphs
- **Graph Representation Learning** — W. Hamilton (free).
- **Networks** — M. Newman.

## Practical
- **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow** — A. Géron.
- **Python Machine Learning** — S. Raschka; **Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn**.
- **Designing Machine Learning Systems** — C. Huyen.
- **Machine Learning Engineering** — A. Burkov (and *The Hundred-Page ML Book*).

## Math
- **Mathematics for Machine Learning** — Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong (free).
- **Linear Algebra and Learning from Data** — G. Strang.
- **Convex Optimization** — Boyd & Vandenberghe (free).
- **Probability and Statistics** — DeGroot & Schervish; **All of Statistics** — Wasserman.
