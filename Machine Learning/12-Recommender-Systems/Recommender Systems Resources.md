# Recommender Systems Resources

## Courses & tutorials
- [Google — Recommendation Systems course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/recommendation) — candidate generation, MF, DNNs, re-ranking (≈4h)
- [Dive into Deep Learning — ch. 21 Recommender Systems](https://d2l.ai/chapter_recommender-systems/index.html) — MF, AutoRec, NCF, sequence-aware, factorization machines with code
- [Stanford CS246 / Mining of Massive Datasets](http://www.mmds.org/) — ch. 9 recommendation systems (free book + slides)
- [fast.ai Practical Deep Learning](https://course.fast.ai/) — collaborative filtering lesson

## Books
- *Recommender Systems: The Textbook* — C. Aggarwal (Springer 2016)
- [*Recommender Systems Handbook*, 3rd ed.](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-0716-2197-4) — Ricci, Rokach, Shapira (2022)
- [*Mining of Massive Datasets*](http://www.mmds.org/) — Leskovec, Rajaraman, Ullman (free)

## Key papers
- Koren, Bell, Volinsky (2009) *Matrix Factorization Techniques for Recommender Systems*, IEEE Computer
- Hu, Koren, Volinsky (2008) *Collaborative Filtering for Implicit Feedback Datasets*
- Rendle et al. (2009) *BPR: Bayesian Personalized Ranking from Implicit Feedback*
- Rendle (2010) *Factorization Machines*
- [Wide & Deep (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.07792) · [NCF (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05031) · [SASRec (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09781) · [BERT4Rec (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.06690) · [DLRM (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00091) · [LightGCN (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.02126)
- Covington, Adams, Sargin (2016) *Deep Neural Networks for YouTube Recommendations*

## Datasets
- [MovieLens (GroupLens)](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/) — the standard benchmark
- Amazon Reviews, Yelp, Last.fm, Netflix Prize, Criteo (CTR), MIND (news)
